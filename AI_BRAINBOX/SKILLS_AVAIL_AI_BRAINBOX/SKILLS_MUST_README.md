### Skills Files

- `RAW_SKILLS_BRAINBOX.md` — raw skills observations and material.
- `PROVEN_SKILLS_BRAINBOX.md` — skills tested and established as proven.
- `REUSABLE_SKILLS_BRAINBOX.md` — skills demonstrated as reusable across appropriate contexts.
- `FAILED_SKILLS_BRAINBOX.md` — failed skills/patterns and their documented lessons.

Skills records should preserve evidence of what was tried, what worked, what failed, and what was learned.
