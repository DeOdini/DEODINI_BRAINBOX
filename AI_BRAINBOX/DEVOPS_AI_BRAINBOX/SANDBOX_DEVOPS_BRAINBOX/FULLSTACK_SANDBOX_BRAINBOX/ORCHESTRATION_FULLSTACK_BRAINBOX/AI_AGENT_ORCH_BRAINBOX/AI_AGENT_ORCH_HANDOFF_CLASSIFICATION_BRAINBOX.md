## Classification

These are proposed role assignments and examples. They do not define a tested handoff procedure, verified tool execution, a transport/interface contract, trigger conditions, approval points, failure handling, or evidence capture. Therefore they are **reference-only and unverified**.

No agent routing rule is activated by this record. No agent capability, connection, or authorization status is inferred. Any future operational handoff record must be separately evidence-backed and remain consistent with CORE/EXE and Governance authorities.
