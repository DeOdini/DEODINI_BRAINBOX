## Sandbox to Production

- Sandbox is where implementations, workflows, tests, and recovery procedures can be attempted, failed, refined, and validated.
- A Sandbox pass does not guarantee Production success. Production is not a “proven forever” destination; it records its own passed, failed, incident, and regression outcomes.
- Promote knowledge or work only when its actual evidence supports the intended status. One successful build alone is not enough to establish broader mastery or production readiness.
- For the FootHive trial, eventual readiness assessment should consider effectiveness, efficiency, repeatability, debugging, maintenance, upgrades, regression handling, uncertainty handling, and readiness. Iterations 02/03 and mastery evidence remain planned until they occur.
- Operator approval is required at the applicable production, release, portfolio, or milestone gate. A technical capability or successful local check does not grant that authority.

## Accurate portfolio claims

When promoting a project into a portfolio or public-facing record, preserve whether it was paid client work or an unpaid spec/trial, and accurately identify the people/agents and workflow involved. Do not imply production adoption or client acceptance without evidence.
